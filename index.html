how it works
  • Live room: your photos go straight to your love's phone, encrypted on the way.
  • To connect you, a room server (PeerJS) and sometimes a Cloudflare relay pass along encrypted data. They can't see your photos.
  • Send to my love as a link: your photos and design are locked (encrypted) on your device first. Our Cloudflare storage keeps only the locked copy for 3 days, then deletes it. The key is inside the link itself, so anyone with the link can open it — only send it to your love.
  • A photo file or code contains your photos, so only send it to your love.
  • The letters come from Google Fonts, so Google can see that this page was opened.
